Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Ozone Capital Finance (“we”, “us”) facilitates and arranges financing through lending institutions. This page explains how we handle information you share through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a funding enquiry, we collect the details you provide: name, mobile number, city, financing requirement, amount range, applicant type, optional description, and consent. We may also receive standard technical data such as browser type if analytics tools are later enabled.

How we use it

Your information is used only to understand and respond to your financing enquiry, to assess a potentially suitable financing route, and to contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about that enquiry. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We may share relevant details with lending institutions only as needed to explore a financing route you have requested. Those institutions process data under their own policies. We may use service providers (for example, a spreadsheet or form endpoint) solely to receive and store enquiries.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal or operational requirements. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; we take reasonable steps to limit access to enquiry data.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your enquiry details, subject to any legal obligation to retain records. Contact us using the phone, WhatsApp, or email published on the website once those details are confirmed.

This website

This landing page may later include Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, or Meta Pixel for conversion measurement. Those tools are not active until tracking IDs are added. This policy does not create a Google Business Profile or any other listing.
